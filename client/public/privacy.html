Privacy Policy

Discord Clone is a small chat app for friends. This page explains what it stores and why.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. We only use these to create your account and show your name and picture to people you chat with.
  • What you create: your username, servers, channels, messages, friend list and friend requests.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We don't show ads or track you across other websites.
  • Voice and video calls go directly between the people in the call and are not recorded or stored.

Who can see your information

Your display name, username and profile picture are visible to other users. Messages are visible to the members of the server they're posted in, or to the other person in a direct message. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a database hosted by our providers (Render and Neon) in the United States.

Deleting your data

To have your account and messages deleted, contact the app owner through the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
